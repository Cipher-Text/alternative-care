Frequently asked questions

Can I switch between monthly and annual billing?
Yes. You can switch at any time. If you switch from monthly to annual mid-cycle, we'll prorate and credit the remaining days. Downgrading from annual to monthly takes effect at the next renewal date.
What happens to my data if I downgrade to Free?
Your data is never deleted. If you exceed the Free plan limits (e.g. more than 30 patients), your existing records remain accessible in read-only mode. New records will be blocked until you upgrade or reduce within limits.
Is there a trial period for Plus or Pro?
Yes. Both Plus and Pro come with a 14-day free trial — no credit card required. At the end of the trial, you'll be asked to add a payment method to continue.
Does the Pro plan support multiple doctors at a clinic?
The Pro plan is per-doctor, with one assistant seat included. For multi-doctor setups, each doctor requires their own subscription. We're building a Clinic plan for teams — contact us for early access pricing.
What payment methods are accepted?
We accept bKash, Nagad, Rocket, and major debit/credit cards (Visa, Mastercard). Bank transfer is available for annual Pro plans on request.
